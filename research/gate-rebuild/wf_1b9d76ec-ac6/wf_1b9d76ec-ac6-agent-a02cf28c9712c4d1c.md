This is a primary academic pre-print (University of Southampton, arXiv q-fin.TR, 12 May 2023) whose core contribution is an agent-based simulator (PRIME/ABIDES), not an entry-edge model — so it touches only two of the six areas in the research question: the slippage/impact cost model and the horizon at which order-flow impact lives.
