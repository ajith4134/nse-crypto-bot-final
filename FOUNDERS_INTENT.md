## ⭐ THE WHY — the Prime Directive (added 2026-07-04)

**This project exists for one reason: to earn the owner a consistent, risk-managed income —
a real, repeatable living, not a lottery or a gamble.** Everything else — all the intelligence,
all 26 pillars — is a means to that end.

The honest truth, always told to the owner: **no system can guarantee profit.** The only sound
path to income is *protect the capital, take small risk-managed edges, and compound.*

**Dual-mode risk (the owner's own rule):**
- **PAPER = the learning lab.** Take unlimited risk. Blow up completely if it teaches us
  something — blowups are training data. No limits on paper. This is where the AI discovers what
  actually makes money.
- **LIVE / real money = protect capital first.** Balanced risk for steady income, every guardrail
  strictly enforced (stop-losses, no full-capital trades, anti-overfit gate, portfolio caps).
  A strategy only reaches real money after it proves consistent profit on paper first.

Judge every decision with the **`/money-lens`** skill before acting.

## The original vision (where it started)

A **CPU-first "network of prediction models with a brain."** Hundreds of ML/AI models wired
together as a graph of "nodes" (each a real model), with an outside **"brain" agent** that can
see all of them, read books/PDFs/news into memory, learn, and figure out how to wire and route
the nodes. Primary use: **trading**, but the models generalize to any data. *(Full text in
`PROJECT_BRIEF.md`.)*

Two principles were set from day one and hold throughout:
1. **Reuse real, working open-source first** — search, copy, adapt, and stitch existing code;
   build from scratch only when nothing exists.
2. **Everything honest and visible** — a real dashboard shows real connections; never fake or
   decorative wiring.
