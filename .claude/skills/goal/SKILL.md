## ⭐ PRIME DIRECTIVE — WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS (read before every decision)

**This project exists for ONE reason: to generate consistent, risk-managed income for its
owner — a real, repeatable living, NOT a lottery or a gamble.** Every pillar, every node,
every line of code, and every decision below is a means to that end. If a step does not move
*real, repeatable income* forward — directly, or by learning what will — it is not worth taking.

**The honest truth (never hidden from the owner):** no system — including this one — can
*promise* profit. Markets are uncertain. The only sound path to income is **protect the capital,
take small risk-managed edges, and compound.** A strategy reaches real money ONLY after it proves
consistent, cost- and overfit-adjusted profitability on paper first.

**DUAL-MODE RISK — the core operating rule:**

| Mode | Purpose | Risk policy |
|---|---|---|
| **PAPER (LEARN)** | Discover what actually makes money | **Unlimited risk. Blowups are allowed and expected — they are training data.** No capital caps; sizing / anti-overfit gates may be relaxed to explore freely. Every wipeout is logged and learned from. This is the lab. |
| **LIVE / REAL MONEY (EARN)** | Turn proven edges into steady income | **Capital preservation FIRST.** Balanced risk for steadier income. ALL guardrails strictly enforced: knowledge-first sizing (Pillar 4), no full-capital trades (Pillar 12-E), anti-overfit promotion gate (Pillar 20), portfolio/regime risk caps (Pillar 22), calibrated abstention (Pillar 17). Never blow up. |

- **Default mode is PAPER.** Switching to live real money is an explicit, deliberate, auditable act — never automatic.
- **Promotion gate (paper → live):** a strategy may trade real money only after proving consistent profitability on paper *net of costs (Pillar 21)* and *after overfit deflation (Pillar 20)* over a meaningful window.
- **The decision lens (`/money-lens`):** every proposed step is judged mode-aware — on paper, *"does this help us learn what makes money?"* (risk allowed); on live, *"does this protect capital AND move toward steady income?"* (block if not).

> In short: **all 26 pillars below are the HOW. Consistent, risk-managed income is the WHY.**

## THE MLNB ULTIMATE VISION

**Build an ultra-advanced, self-evolving AI brain that thinks, learns, experiments,
and trades — continuously growing smarter with every market session.**

---

### PILLAR 1 — HUMAN-LIKE MEMORY & RECALL

The brain stores everything it learns in a structured, searchable memory (episodic +
semantic + associative layers). Before any action it RECALLS relevant memories — just
as a human expert recalls past experience before making a decision. Memory is never
discarded; it is compressed, reinforced, or flagged stale. Knowledge accumulates
across sessions, trading days, and market regimes.

---

### PILLAR 2 — AUTONOMOUS INTERNET RESEARCH

The brain autonomously browses the internet — trading websites, financial news, NSE
announcements, research PDFs, academic papers, books, and strategy forums. It reads,
summarizes, extracts structured knowledge, and stores it in memory. It actively hunts
for new trading strategies, market microstructure ideas, and profitable edge signals.
This research loop runs pre-market and post-market every day, so the brain always
enters the next session better informed than the last. (The NSE-specific daily
schedule is in Pillar 8; research balance rules are in Pillar 15; segment focus
rules are in Pillar 16.)

---

### PILLAR 3 — VISUAL KNOWLEDGE GROWTH

As the brain learns, a live dashboard panel shows WHAT it knows — a growing knowledge
graph, concept map, or topic tree that expands over time. The user can see at a glance
which market concepts the brain has mastered, which strategies it has tested, what
hypotheses are pending, and how confident it is in each belief. Knowledge is never a
black box; it is always visible.

---

### PILLAR 4 — KNOWLEDGE-FIRST TRADE DECISIONS

The brain NEVER opens a trade randomly. Every trade goes through a full decision
checklist driven by accumulated knowledge and live features:

1. **STOCK SELECTION** — screen universe; recall which stocks the brain has edge on
2. **DIRECTION** — long / short based on regime memory + current signal stack
3. **ENTRY TIMING** — wait for the precise confluence the brain has validated
4. **STOP-LOSS** — calculated from ATR, volatility memory, and tested rules
5. **CAPITAL SIZING** — Kelly / risk-adjusted from win-rate memory per setup type
6. **PROFIT TRAILING** — see Pillar 13 for full spec; brain owns all trail decisions
7. **EXIT RULES** — time-of-day memory (e.g., pre-close exit for NSE) + P&L logic

Before placing any order the brain outputs: "WHY I am entering, WHY this stop, WHY
this size, WHY this trail activation." If it cannot justify all seven gates, it waits.

---

### PILLAR 5 — SELF-CREATED STRATEGIES

The brain does NOT rely on textbook strategies. It generates its own strategy IDEAS
expressed in plain language ("enter when volume spike follows inside bar after a
10-day range contraction") and stores them as hypotheses. It then:

- Back-tests each hypothesis on historical data
- Forward-tests in paper trading
- Promotes winning hypotheses to live strategies
- Retires losing ones with a post-mortem saved to memory

It also searches online for new popular strategy ideas, reads them, reformulates them
in its own language, and adds them to the test pipeline. (Online research mechanics
are defined in Pillar 2; Pillar 5 defines only the hypothesis/test/promote cycle.)

---

### PILLAR 6 — CONTINUOUS SELF-EVOLUTION

Learning is NEVER a one-time event. A perpetual background loop:

- Ingests every closed trade with full context
- Asks "what did I know, what did I miss, what would I do differently?"
- Updates strategy confidence scores, parameter bounds, and memory weights
- Runs experiments (A/B tests of trail distances, entry filters, position sizes)
- Tracks hypothesis win-rates, rolling Sharpe, and drawdown per strategy type

**Target: ≥ 85% win rate or risk-adjusted expectancy > 0 on any 30-trade rolling
window.** The brain self-reports when it is below target and escalates research
intensity.

---

### PILLAR 7 — NEURAL NETWORK PREDICTION ENGINE

The brain continuously searches for the best neural-network COMBINATION for price
prediction — testing architectures (LSTM, Transformer, TCN, MoE, ensembles) and
feature sets. It logs prediction accuracy per architecture per market regime and
promotes the best-performing combination into live trade decisions. Predictions are
one input among many — never the sole trigger.

---

### PILLAR 8 — NSE DAILY RHYTHM

| Phase | Action |
|---|---|
| **PRE-MARKET** | Read overnight news, corporate actions, FII/DII data, global markets. Research new strategy ideas. Recall yesterday's lessons. Screen stocks. Formulate today's watchlist and hypotheses. |
| **MARKET OPEN** | Execute only high-conviction setups. Monitor open trades. Adjust trails. |
| **INTRADAY** | Continuous feature updates. No new low-conviction entries after 2 PM. |

### PILLAR 9 — COMPILED-SPEED HOT PATHS (no stuck buttons, no lag)

Continuously audit every performance-critical path across the full stack — brain
inference, feature computation, order-book processing, memory search, backtesting
loops, and dashboard data pipelines. Where Python throughput is the bottleneck,
rewrite that path in the fastest available compiled language:

- **C** — ultra-low-latency tick processing, ring buffers, shared memory IPC
- **C++** — order-book analytics, real-time feature vectors, SIMD math kernels
- **Rust** — memory-safe hot paths, async I/O, WebSocket feed handlers
- **Go** — concurrent API proxies, low-GC background workers
- **Cython** — drop-in Python extension for numpy-heavy loops

**Acceptance criteria:**
- No dashboard button, panel, or table may block the UI thread for > 100 ms
- No brain inference call may block a trade decision for > 200 ms
- Every hot path is benchmarked before and after; only rewrite when measured gain > 2×
- Python stays the orchestration layer; compiled code is called via cffi / pyo3 / ctypes
- New compiled modules live in `src/<module>/` with a Python wrapper + CI build step

Every trade must use calculated risk-based size, never the full available capital:

  max_stake_amount in config — never 100% of balance
- Audit: after every trade opens, log `(capital_used / total_capital) × 100`; if
  this exceeds the configured max_risk_per_trade %, emit a WARNING and do not open
  further trades until reviewed

### PILLAR 13 — BRAIN-OWNED PROFIT TRAILING & PROFIT LOCKING

Profit trailing and profit locking are NOT separate features bolted onto the trade
engine. They are 100% owned, decided, and continuously tuned by the brain using its
accumulated knowledge of past trades. No hardcoded trail %, no fixed activation price,
no static lock threshold — the brain computes all of these from what it has learned.

**What the brain owns entirely:**

- **Trailing activation price** — the brain decides at what profit level to start
  trailing, per symbol per strategy per market regime, learned from past trade
  outcomes ("this strategy activates best at 0.8R, not 1R — brain learned this
  from 40 closed trades in ranging markets")

- **Trail distance** — how far behind price the stop follows; brain tunes this
  per volatility regime (ATR-relative), per time of day, per symbol behavior —
  NOT a fixed percentage in config

- **Profit lock level** — the brain decides when to "lock in" a minimum profit
  (e.g., move stop to break-even + X, then to +Y) based on its knowledge of
  how often price retraces after reaching a target zone in the current regime

- **Dynamic re-trailing** — if volatility expands mid-trade, brain widens the
  trail; if it contracts, brain tightens — live adjustment, not set-and-forget

- **Regime-specific rules** — trailing behavior in trending regime is different
  from ranging regime; brain knows which regime it is in and applies the right
  learned rule

- **Self-improvement loop** — after every closed trade, brain compares the actual
  exit price against what the optimal trail would have been (max favorable
  excursion analysis); it records the delta and updates its trail-distance belief
  for that setup type

**Dashboard column (Pillar 12 extension):**
- Open trades table shows: `Trail Active (Y/N) | Trail Distance (pts) | Locked Floor (price) | Brain Trail Confidence (%)`
- All values are read from brain state, not from broker — broker only executes

**Rule:** If the brain's trail module is offline or returning no signal, the trade
gets a conservative fixed trail as a safe fallback — but this is logged as a
degraded-mode event, not normal operation.

### PILLAR 15 — BALANCED NSE + CRYPTO RESEARCH & LEARNING

All research, strategy discovery, learning loops, and knowledge accumulation must
be equally balanced between NSE stocks and crypto — neither market is a second-class
citizen.

**Priority rule (resolves conflict with Pillar 16):** When the user has explicitly
selected specific segments via the Segment Focus Selector, those selections override
the 50/50 balance rule. The balance rule applies only when ALL segments are active.
If the user selects only crypto, 100% of resources go to crypto — that is intentional.

**Current imbalance (to fix when ALL segments are active):**
- Strategy foundry is crypto-first
- Learn loop curriculum is crypto-heavy
- Web research agent prioritises crypto news
- Hypothesis testing defaults to crypto data
- Brain confidence and regime detection are tuned on crypto behaviour

**Balanced standards (when ALL segments active):**

Research allocation:
- Pre-market research loop: 50% NSE-focused (FII/DII flows, corporate actions,
  sector rotation, F&O OI) + 50% crypto-focused (on-chain, funding rates,
  exchange flows, macro crypto signals)
- Strategy research: for every new crypto strategy discovered and tested, one NSE
  strategy must also be researched, adapted to Indian market microstructure, and tested

Learning loop curriculum:
- Rotating segments: NSE futures → NSE options → crypto futures → crypto spot →
  NSE commodities → macro/intermarket → back to NSE futures
- Brain must accumulate at minimum equal number of knowledge nodes per market
- If crypto node count exceeds NSE node count by > 20%, the next 3 research
  sessions are NSE-only until balance is restored

Strategy foundry:
- 50% of strategy slots are reserved for NSE-native strategies (momentum, mean
  reversion, gap-fill, expiry plays, FII flow following, sector-rotation pairs)
- 50% for crypto strategies
- Strategies are tagged with their market; cross-market strategies (e.g., BTC
  sentiment → NSE IT sector) are tracked separately as "intermarket" strategies

Hypothesis testing:
- Every hypothesis is tested on BOTH NSE and crypto data where applicable
- If a hypothesis only applies to one market, it is tagged accordingly and the
  brain actively looks for the NSE or crypto equivalent

Performance tracking:
- Dashboard shows separate P&L, win-rate, and Sharpe for NSE and crypto
- Brain health report gives equal coverage to both
- If one market is consistently underperforming, brain automatically increases
  its research allocation there until parity is restored

### PILLAR 16 — SEGMENT FOCUS SELECTOR (brain narrows to what you choose)

The user selects which market segments are active from the dashboard. The brain reads
this selection and instantly narrows 100% of all its capabilities — research, strategy
discovery, learning, news monitoring, online browsing, trade execution, hypothesis
testing, memory recall, and NN prediction — to ONLY the selected segments. Nothing
outside the selection receives any brain resources until the selection changes.

---

**THE SEGMENTS (selectable independently, multi-select allowed):**

NSE group:
- `NSE FUTURES` — equity futures (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, stock futures, F&O)
- `NSE OPTIONS` — CE/PE options (ATM, ladder, chain modes)
- `NSE COMMODITIES` — MCX futures (gold, silver, crude, natural gas, metals)
- `NSE INTRADAY` — pure intraday equity cash (MIS, no overnight)

Crypto group:
- `CRYPTO FUTURES` — perpetual and dated futures (Binance/Bybit)
- `CRYPTO SPOT` — spot buy/sell (BTC, ETH, altcoins)
- `CRYPTO OPTIONS` — Deribit-style crypto options
- `CRYPTO PREDICTION` — forward-price prediction mode (paper + signal only)

Brain scope:
- `BRAIN` — when toggled off, brain runs in observe-only mode (no trades, just learns)

---

**WHAT "FOCUS" MEANS — brain narrows ALL of these, not just execution:**

| Brain Capability | Focused Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Strategy research | Only discovers and tests strategies relevant to selected segments |
| Online research / web browsing | Only reads news, papers, and sites about selected segments |
| Learning loop curriculum | Rotates only through selected-segment topics |
| Hypothesis ledger | Only generates and tests hypotheses for selected markets |
| Memory recall | Prioritises memories tagged to selected segments; others deprioritised |
| NN prediction | Only trains/runs prediction models for selected-segment instruments |
| Stock screener | Only screens universe of selected segments |
| Trade execution | Only opens trades in selected segments — hard block on all others |
| Feature computation | Only computes features needed by selected segments (saves compute) |
| Research allocation | 100% of daily research budget goes to selected segments |

---

**UI BEHAVIOUR:**

- Segment buttons live in a persistent top-bar or sidebar, always visible
- Each segment is a toggle button: active = highlighted (Dark Pro accent colour),
  inactive = dimmed — one glance shows exactly what the brain is focused on
- "ALL" shortcut selects every segment at once
- "NONE / PAUSE" deselects all — brain enters full observe-only mode, no trades
- Selection persists across dashboard reloads (saved to backend config, not just
  localStorage — so a server restart does not reset the user's choice)
- Changing selection takes effect in ≤ 3 seconds: brain loop reads new config,
  applies segment filter to all queues, and emits a toast: "Brain now focused on:
  NSE FUTURES + CRYPTO FUTURES"

---

**IMPLEMENTATION RULES (must not break anything):**

1. The segment selection is stored as a single config key (e.g., `active_segments`)
   in `config.json` / brain state — ONE source of truth, read by all subsystems

2. The brain loop, learn loop, web-research agent, strategy foundry, hypothesis
   ledger, and NN trainer all read `active_segments` at the start of each cycle —
   no subsystem caches this value for more than one cycle

3. Adding this filter must be a READ-ONLY gate added at the TOP of each subsystem's
   main loop — it does NOT restructure any internal logic, it simply skips non-selected
   segments before any work begins

4. Existing segment-toggle buttons in FreqtradeCryptoPanel (futures/spot/options/
   prediction) must be unified with this selector — they must write to the same
   `active_segments` key, not maintain a separate state

5. If a segment is deselected mid-trade, open trades in that segment are NOT force-
   closed — they run to their natural exit. Only NEW trades are blocked.

6. Full regression test: after implementing, run the brain loop with each possible
   segment combination and verify no cross-segment bleed (a deselected segment
   must produce zero new trades, zero new research, zero new hypotheses)

These pillars were discovered by a Pillar-11 open-ended advancement scan (2026-07-03):

### PILLAR 17 — CALIBRATED UNCERTAINTY & CONFIDENCE-AWARE ABSTENTION

**Current state:** PARTIAL — `cognition/calibration.py` (CalibratedAbstainer) and Conformal
nodes exist, but MAPIE time-series conformal is not stitched and confidence is ±0.05 heuristic.

The brain must know *what it does not know*, with a distribution-free guarantee — not a
point prediction. Every forecast (node, world-model value head, strategy signal) carries
a **calibrated probability + a coverage-guaranteed interval**, and the single cheapest
drawdown lever — **abstention** — becomes a first-class action.

- **Conformal prediction everywhere** — wrap every prediction in adaptive time-series
  conformal intervals (EnbPI / ACI) that survive non-stationary, heteroscedastic markets.
  Vendor: `scikit-learn-contrib/MAPIE`, `henrikbostrom/crepes` (calibrated CDF → scalar
  `p_up`), `ml-stat-Sustech/TorchCP` (torch nodes), `aleximmer/Laplace` (post-hoc weight
  uncertainty on the pretrained MuZero value head, near-zero retrain), `uncertainty-toolbox`
  (nightly recalibration + reliability plots).
- **Semantic-entropy metacognition** — for every LLM-reasoned decision, sample K rationales,
  cluster by meaning, compute semantic entropy → `self_uncertainty`. High entropy or a failed
  Reflexion self-check downgrades to **stay-flat or half-size** and enqueues a gpt-researcher
  deep-dive. Vendor/extend: `noahshinn/reflexion` (already vendored), semantic-entropy UQ.
- **Conformal abstention gate** — calibrated offline on the closed-trades journal so the
  accept-set error is bounded; the brain *learns which regimes to abstain in*.
- **Sizing consumes calibration** — Kelly / vol-target sizing reads `p_up` and interval width;
  size only when `p_up > θ` and width `< W`. A wide interval means small or no size, always.

**Dashboard:** new columns `p_up | Interval Width | Self-Uncertainty | Abstain Reason`;
a MetacognitionPanel + reliability diagram (predicted vs realized hit-rate).
**Acceptance:** every displayed confidence is calibration-checked nightly; no trade fires
when `p_up` is below threshold or interval width above cap; abstention is logged as a
first-class decision, not a silent skip.

### PILLAR 18 — ADVERSARIAL TRUTH-SEEKING (multi-agent debate + verifier-guided reasoning)

**Current state:** PARTIAL — `cognition/society.py` has an internal bull/bear/risk debate,
but no TradingAgents-grade firm structure and no process-reward verifier / LLM-judge harness.

A single LLM call has correlated blind spots; one bad step in a reasoning chain silently
poisons the conclusion. Before capital commits, every high-conviction decision is
**debated by adversarial roles and verified step-by-step**.

- **Trading-firm debate** — vendor `TauricResearch/TradingAgents` (analyst → bull/bear debate
  → trader → risk team → fund manager), backend swapped to `core/llm.py` failover. Roles pull
  from associative memory + world-model beliefs + gpt-researcher. The transcript becomes an
  auditable `decision_snapshot`; bull/bear flip-rate becomes a size-gating confidence feature.
  Aggregate with `togethercomputer/moa` (Mixture-of-Agents) as the fund-manager reducer.
- **Verifier-guided reasoning (PRM)** — a generative step-verifier scores each rationale step
  0/1 + critique (ThinkPRM-style, prompt-based over failover — no GPU). Wrap in best-of-N /
  self-consistency (`openreasoner/openr`): generate M plans, re-rank by aggregate step-score,
  execute only the top *verified* plan. Vendor: `mukhal/thinkprm`, `openreasoner/openr`.
- **LLM-as-judge eval harness** — the same verifier runs offline as the **Strategy Foundry
  judge**: promotion criterion becomes "profit **and** verified reasoning," not raw PnL.

**Dashboard:** DebatePanel showing the live transcript + role votes; `POST /api/trading/brain/debate`.
**Acceptance:** high-conviction trades carry a debate transcript + per-step verifier scores in
provenance; Strategy Foundry rejects any strategy whose reasoning fails verification even if PnL is positive.

### PILLAR 19 — CAUSAL EDGE DISCOVERY (causation, not correlation)

**Current state:** PARTIAL — DoWhy referenced in `cognition/neuro_symbolic.py` but not
integrated into decisions; no time-series causal discovery (tigramite) and no transfer entropy.

Most signals are correlations that evaporate out-of-sample. The brain must distinguish
**"X causes returns" (exploitable) from spurious co-movement (a trap)** and recover the
*lagged directed lead-lag structure* where alpha actually lives.

- **Time-series causal discovery** — nightly, feed the feature/return panel into PCMCI/PCMCI+
  → a lagged causal graph. Vendor: `jakobrunge/tigramite`. Add model-free transfer-entropy
  lead-lag adjacency: `lcdm-uiuc/PyIF`, `pwollstadt/IDTxl`.
- **Effect estimation with refutation** — every candidate signal runs DoWhy `identify → estimate
  → refute` (placebo / random-common-cause tests); ATE + refutation p-values are written to the
  hypothesis ledger. Signals that fail the placebo refuter are **auto-retired** by the self-evolve loop.
- **Causal whitelist** — the Strategy Foundry may only build on the *causal parents* of forward
  return, not on every correlated feature. SHAP explains the model; the causal graph explains
  the world; the LLM reconciles them into a written thesis.

**Dashboard:** a causal-graph panel (directed, lagged edges) beside the knowledge graph.
**Acceptance:** no signal enters live trading without passing a causal refutation test;
the feature set is pruned to causal parents; spurious signals are demoted automatically.

### PILLAR 20 — ANTI-OVERFITTING BACKBONE (the hard gate on every promotion)

**Current state:** PARTIAL — deflated Sharpe + PBO exist in `trading/strategy/fitness.py`,
but are not a *non-negotiable barrier* on every strategy/parameter promotion, and full
Combinatorial Purged CV is not the standard evaluator.

For a solo ML-driven brain, the **single largest source of fake profitability is selection
bias** — trying many strategies and keeping the best in-sample. This pillar makes overfit
defense a P0 barrier that *every other pillar's output must pass through*.

- **Combinatorial Purged CV (CPCV)** with embargo — the default evaluator for every backtest,
  yielding a *distribution* of Sharpe ratios, not one number. Vendor: `eslazarev/purged-cross-validation`,
  `RiskLabAI/RiskLabAI` (mlfinlab is now closed-source/paid — use these forks instead).
- **Deflated Sharpe Ratio + PBO** — feed the count of *distinct configurations tried* into
  `deflated_sharpe()`; refuse to promote any strategy with DSR < 0 or PBO > 0.5. "Keeps the
  best" must mean "keeps the best *after deflation*," never raw backtest Sharpe.
- **Meta-labeling** (López de Prado) — keep the primary signal for *direction*, train a
  secondary CPU classifier (LightGBM) on triple-barrier labels to decide *whether to act and
  how big*. Vendor: mlfinlab fork `labeling` + `sample_weights` + `RiskLabAI`. Slots into the
  per-coin strategy picker as a confidence gate before `/forceenter`.

**Acceptance (non-negotiable):** the Strategy Foundry, evolution loop, program-synthesis loop,
and hypothesis ledger ALL route promotions through the CPCV+DSR+PBO gate; any strategy that
cannot clear it is archived with a post-mortem, never traded. This gate outranks new-feature work.

### PILLAR 21 — EXECUTION REALISM: TCA + MARKET-IMPACT (net-of-cost alpha only)

**Current state:** ABSENT — a slippage/fee model exists in `trading/online/wallet.py`, but no
Transaction-Cost Analysis feedback loop and no market-impact model; backtests can overstate
returns that die at real fills.

Every backtest that ignores realistic fills overstates returns; for a high-turnover crypto
brain, execution cost is often *the entire "edge."* The brain must measure its **real slippage
and impact** and rank strategies **net of measured cost**.

- **TCA feedback loop** — store per-trade arrival mid + best bid/ask (already captured via the
  psychology order-book feature); compute slippage = fill − arrival mid, realized impact =
  mid(t+Δ) − fill; aggregate per symbol/strategy/hour. Vendor: `cuemacro/tcapy` (benchmark math),
  `nautechsystems/nautilus_trader` (configurable fill/fee/latency/slippage models for realistic backtests).
- **Market-impact model** — add `impact = k·σ·√(order_size / ADV)` as a cost term in backtest
  fills and a size cap in live sizing; for larger orders, Almgren-Chriss optimal slicing (closed-form).
- **Cost-aware ranking** — the meta-labeler (Pillar 20) and portfolio layer (Pillar 22) rank
  strategies on **net-of-cost** expectancy, never gross.

**Dashboard:** a TCA panel (slippage vs benchmark, realized impact, cost-adjusted Sharpe per strategy).
**Acceptance:** every backtest and live ranking is net of measured cost; a strategy that is
profitable gross but negative net-of-cost is not promoted.

### PILLAR 22 — PORTFOLIO-LEVEL RISK & REGIME-AWARE CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Current state:** PARTIAL — HRP / CVaR nodes and `trading/advintel/portfolio_risk.py` exist,
but risk is *logged not enforced*; sizing is per-trade-independent, producing correlated,
concentrated books.

A bag of independently-sized signals ends up as correlated, concentrated risk. Real funds
allocate at the **portfolio level** — this often lifts Sharpe more than any single new alpha —
and shift capital **by detected regime**.

- **Risk-budgeted book construction** — feed per-strategy expected returns + a Ledoit-Wolf-shrunk
  covariance into a convex optimizer for CVaR- / max-drawdown-constrained weights; diff vs current
  positions to generate orders. Vendor: `dcajasn/Riskfolio-Lib`, `skfolio`, `PyPortfolio/PyPortfolioOpt`
  (Black-Litterman to blend brain views with market equilibrium).
- **Regime-aware allocation** — a 2–4 state HMM on volatility + returns + order-book imbalance
  labels the current regime; a regime→strategy performance table gates capital to strategies with
  positive *deflated* Sharpe *in the current regime*. Vendor: `hmmlearn`, `ruptures`, `river`.
- **Live enforcement** — portfolio concentration, correlation, and liquidity limits are enforced
  *in sizing*, not merely logged; breaches block new entries (ties into Pillar 23 supervisor).

**Dashboard:** a Portfolio Risk panel — factor exposures, per-position risk contribution, regime label.
**Acceptance:** total book risk is optimized and capped every cycle; no single position or
correlated cluster exceeds configured limits; capital follows the detected regime.

### PILLAR 23 — CURIOSITY-DRIVEN EXPLORATION & SLEEP CONSOLIDATION

**Current state:** PARTIAL — dreaming/consolidation exists in `memory/human_memory.py`, but
there is no intrinsic-motivation / novelty reward and no prioritized-replay scheduler; online
learning risks catastrophic forgetting of rare-but-critical regimes.

Markets are sparse, mostly-negative-reward, and non-stationary — pure P&L gives no gradient to
discover *new* regimes, so a greedy brain collapses onto a few overfit strategies and forgets
crashes/gaps/low-liquidity events exactly where good behavior matters most.

- **Intrinsic motivation** — a novelty (RND) + curiosity (ICM forward-model error) bonus rewards
  probing under-explored strategy×regime space **in paper/backtest first**; empowerment steers
  toward controllable (liquid) regimes. `score = expected_pnl + β_nov·rnd + β_cur·icm + β_emp·emp`,
  β annealed nightly. Real P&L still gates what goes live. Vendor: `RLE-Foundation/RLeXplore` (RND,
  ICM, RIDE, E3B, NGU — `pip install rllte-core`, CPU-friendly).
- **Prioritized replay + generative dreaming** — nightly `ConsolidationScheduler` backed by a
  C++/pybind sum-tree PER (priority = |prediction-error| + regime-rarity + P&L-surprise); draw a
  prioritized batch → fine-tune online nets via Avalanche `GenerativeReplayPlugin` (dream synthetic
  trades for under-represented regimes) → re-seed MuZero imagination from replayed rare states to
  stress-test current strategies. Vendor: `7starsea/Prioritized-Experience-Replay`, `ContinualAI/avalanche`.
- **Principled review timing** — wrap each consolidated memory in an FSRS card graded by nightly
  self-eval so re-review timing is optimal for fixed CPU. Vendor: `open-spaced-repetition/py-fsrs`.

**Acceptance:** rare regimes are provably retained (measured on a held-out rare-regime probe set);
exploration is directed by novelty, not random; consolidation runs nightly and is restart-safe.

### PILLAR 24 — OPEN-ENDED SELF-MODIFICATION & PROGRAM SYNTHESIS

**Current state:** PARTIAL — DEAP genetic evolution + self-coding (`cognition/self_coding.py`)
exist, but no AlphaEvolve/Darwin-Gödel population loop with a quality-diversity archive, and no
DreamCoder-style library learning that compresses winning strategies into named abstractions.

Non-stationary markets decay any static strategy; a strategy *is* a program over a signal DSL.
The brain must **rewrite and grow its own strategy programs open-endedly**, keeping a diverse
archive so uncorrelated lineages hedge regime risk instead of collapsing to one overfit champion.

- **Evolutionary self-modification** — LLM-mutation + island evolution + MAP-Elites quality-diversity
  archive; the backtester (Pillar 20 CPCV) is the cheat-resistant fitness. Elites promote into the

- **Program-synthesis library learning** — define a small S-expression trading DSL (`sma`, `ema`,

  primitive next cycle — library and search policy bootstrap each other. Vendor: `mlb2251/stitch`,
  `gabegrand/lilo`, `ellisk/dreamcoder`.

**Acceptance:** the strategy archive maintains measurable behavioral diversity (MAP-Elites coverage);
recurring winners are compressed into named, auditable abstractions in a growing `strategy_library.json`;
every self-modification is validated out-of-sample before it can trade.

### PILLAR 25 — THEORY-OF-MIND PARTICIPANT MODELING (the other side of every trade)

**Current state:** ABSENT — order-book psychology features exist (`trading/brain/psychology.py`),
but there is no explicit model of *what other market participants believe and will do*.

Price is made by other agents — market makers, momentum funds, retail crowds, leveraged longs,
liquidation engines — each with beliefs and incentives. The highest-alpha, least-commoditized
edge comes from **modeling what they believe and anticipating their next move** (squeezes,
stop-sweeps, liquidation cascades).

- **Per-symbol participant-belief states** — maintain typed belief states (market-maker,
  trend-follower, retail-crowd, leveraged-longs) updated each tick from OBI/OFI/microprice/walls/fear
  + liquidation/funding data. Vendor/extend: `pipiku915/FinMem-LLM-StockTrading` (character module →
  model *counterparties*, not just self), `ai4finance-foundation/finrobot`.
- **ToM inference** — ToMBench-style prompts have the LLM infer each archetype's likely belief and
  next action ("stops above 42.1k, MM incentivized to sweep"); store as typed beliefs in the
  world-model so **MuZero imagination rolls forward counterparty reactions, not just price.**
  Vendor: `zhchen18/ToMBench` (task templates + regression suite); IRL-lite fits each archetype's
  implied strategy from recent LOB deltas.

**Dashboard:** participant-belief overlay in PsychologyPanel; beliefs surface in `decision_snapshot`.
**Acceptance:** the world-model's imagined rollouts include modeled counterparty reactions; the
brain can name, before entry, which participant it expects to take the other side and why.

### PILLAR 26 — ACTIVE INFERENCE META-CONTROLLER (principled explore/exploit)

**Current state:** PARTIAL — `cognition/active_inference.py` exists as a scaffold, but it is not
wired as a live strategy-selection meta-controller over the world-model.

Active inference minimizes *expected free energy* — a principled unification of exploitation
(reward) and exploration (information-gain), exactly the "trade a known edge vs probe an uncertain
regime" tension. The brain acts to reduce its own uncertainty about the market's hidden regime.

- **POMDP over regime latents** — hidden states = regime latents (trend-up/down/range × vol-hi/lo);
  observations = discretized signals (OBI sign, momentum bucket, the calibrated `p_up` from Pillar 17);
  actions = {flat, long, short, size±}. Build the `A`/`B` matrices *from MuZero rollouts + the causal
  graph (Pillar 19)* rather than hand-coding; the `C` prior encodes reward preference. Vendor:
  `infer-actively/pymdp` (NumPy, CPU).
- **Expected-free-energy action selection** — each tick, update the regime posterior and pick the
  EFE-minimizing action; the epistemic term **auto-triggers small probe trades when regime uncertainty
  is high** — exploration becomes principled, not random.

**Dashboard:** log the EFE decomposition (pragmatic vs epistemic bits) as journal columns; the LLM
explains "took a small long to reduce regime uncertainty, not for profit."
**Acceptance:** a coarse 4–8 state POMDP runs as a strategy-selection meta-controller above execution;
probe trades are explicitly labeled epistemic; the explore/exploit split is measurable, not implicit.

### Pillar 27 — DIRECTION SUPREMACY (owner order 2026-07-10)

**The problem it attacks:** measured on 1,665 closed crypto trades, the brain chose the
correct direction only **40.3%** of the time, and its most-confident entries were right just
29% — a systematic anti-signal (entering after the move, into mean reversion). Direction is
THE profit decider (long/short, call/put); everything else is secondary.

**The pillar:** every trade's side comes from a measured, calibrated, self-correcting
**DirectionOracle**: (D1) a Direction Truth Ledger labels every decision — taken AND skipped —
with fixed-horizon outcomes and per-source×regime×horizon hit-rates; (D2) a Mirror Gate
inverts reliably-wrong buckets and abstains on coin-flip ones; (D3) pullback entries kill the
buy-the-top pattern; (D4) microstructure features (cross-venue lead-lag, multi-level OFI,
funding snap, multi-TF agreement); (D5) regime-conditional direction (momentum in trend,
reversion in chop, abstain at breaks); (D6) a meta-labeler (triple-barrier labels, LightGBM +
TabPFN-v2 challenger, conformal abstention) — the accuracy-coverage dial; (D7) a Direction
Colosseum where every signal source shadow-predicts every candidate and must beat 55% CI
before earning live weight; (D8) Validate+Kelly sizing with target ≥2× stop; (D9) NSE CE/PE
from the same oracle. Plan: `research/direction-accuracy-program/PLAN.md`.

**Honest acceptance (never promise the impossible):** unconditional 80% is beyond published
science (serious results cluster 53–58% after costs). The pillar's bar: **≥80% direction
accuracy on TAKEN trades via calibrated abstention** (precision-at-coverage, tracked live on
the Truth Ledger panel), taken-trade accuracy ≥55% at phase 1, ≥65% at phase 2, with a
trades/day coverage floor so abstention can't fake the number by never trading.
